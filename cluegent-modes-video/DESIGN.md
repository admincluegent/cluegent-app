## Style Prompt

Create a cinematic dark product video for Cluegent using real UI screenshots. The film should feel like a premium desktop AI launch trailer: dark workspace, soft depth, focused cursor actions, elegant zooms, and clear labels. The product UI must remain the hero in every scene.

## Colors

- Background: `#05070a`
- Surface: `#0d1317`
- Foreground: `#f4f7f3`
- Muted text: `#a6b2ad`
- Primary accent: `#69f2b3`
- Blue action: `#1596ff`
- Warm accent: `#f4b441`
- Border: `#1f342b`

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, system sans-serif
- Use bold, compact titles and smaller functional labels.

## Motion

- Use cursor clicks, soft zooms, drag/move motion, translucent spotlight overlays, scan sweeps, and staggered captions.
- Every screenshot should ease in with `power3.out` or `expo.out`.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not replace the screenshots with mockups.
- Do not overuse generic neon gradients.
- Do not crop away the user actions described in the prompt.
- Do not add distracting text blocks over the important UI.
