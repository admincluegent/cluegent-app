# Cluegent Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

## No Refunds

Cluegent does not provide refunds once a subscription is purchased.

This includes partial billing periods, unused time, accidental purchases, plan changes, cancellation after purchase, or non-use of the service.

## Immediate Cancellation

When you cancel a Cluegent subscription, cancellation takes effect immediately. Paid plan access may stop immediately, and your account may return to free trial or free plan limits.

Cancellation does not continue paid access until the end of the billing period.

## Payment Processor

Payments and subscription billing are processed by Razorpay. Payment method handling, bank authorization, and payment records are managed by Razorpay according to its policies and applicable law.
