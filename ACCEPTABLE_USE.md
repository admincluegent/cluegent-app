# Cluegent Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: May 8, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy explains how Cluegent may and may not be used. It applies to the Cluegent desktop app, website, backend services, and related features.

## Allowed Use

You may use Cluegent to support lawful, permitted workflows such as:

- meeting notes and summaries;
- technical explanations;
- interview preparation where allowed;
- coding help;
- screenshot-based analysis;
- personal productivity and learning.

## Prohibited Use

You may not use Cluegent to:

- violate laws, regulations, workplace rules, school rules, platform rules, or meeting consent requirements;
- secretly record, transcribe, screenshot, or process conversations where consent or disclosure is required and has not been obtained;
- cheat in exams, interviews, assessments, or hiring processes where AI assistance is prohibited;
- impersonate another person or misrepresent your use of AI;
- generate or request illegal, harmful, abusive, deceptive, infringing, or exploitative content;
- attempt to bypass subscriptions, usage limits, authentication, payment systems, or backend security;
- overload, scrape, reverse engineer, or attack Cluegent systems;
- share, sell, or transfer account access without permission.

## Enforcement

We may limit, suspend, or terminate access if we believe usage violates this policy, creates legal risk, harms users or third parties, causes payment or fraud risk, or threatens service reliability.
