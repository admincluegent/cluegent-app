Steps to use Cluegent:

1. Download and install Cluegent.
2. Open Cluegent and sign in.
3. Click Start Cluegent.
4. Join your interview or meeting on Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams.
5. Click the toggle Undetectable to stay invisible during screen share.
6. Click Start listening so Cluegent can listen to the conversation.
7. When you need help, click Answer or press Ctrl + Enter.
8. For coding or screen questions, click Screenshot or press Ctrl + [.
9. You can also type your own question in the chat box and submit it.
10. Use quick actions like Clarify, Brainstorm, or Follow Up Question for better answers.
11. To personalize answers, go to Settings → Customize and add your resume/context.
