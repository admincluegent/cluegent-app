## Evidence and limits

Free tools used: Google Search Console (28 days), Google Autocomplete (`suggestqueries.google.com`, English), and public search results and official competitor pages. Search Console showed 210 clicks, 15.4k impressions, 1.4% CTR and average position 14.5. The query `parakeet ai` had 1,360 impressions and two clicks; `chiku ai shortcuts` had 18 impressions and nine clicks. These are Cluegent's observed metrics, not competitor traffic estimates. The report showed 795 queries, with some data potentially omitted by Google for privacy.

Autocomplete returned all twenty target phrases or close question-and-answer variants. It validates phrasing, not monthly volume or low difficulty. No verified monthly search-volume figures were obtained. Do not label these keywords as proven high-volume or promise top rankings. The existing site already covers competitor reviews, prices and alternatives extensively; this batch expands into distinct preparation intents rather than repeating those pages.
