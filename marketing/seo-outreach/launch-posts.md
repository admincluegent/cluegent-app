## Product Hunt Description

Cluegent is a desktop AI assistant for permitted live interviews, coding rounds, and technical calls.

It helps candidates stay structured under pressure with live context, typed prompts, screenshot-aware answers, resume-aware customization, and quick actions. Instead of switching between your meeting, editor, browser, and a generic chatbot, Cluegent keeps support inside a private desktop overlay.

Useful for:

- Coding interviews
- Freshers and job seekers
- Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams interviews
- Resume-aware project answers
- Screenshot-based coding and debugging questions
- Permitted technical meetings

Important: Cluegent should be used only where AI assistance, transcription, screenshots, and desktop overlays are allowed.

Website: https://www.cluegent.com/
