# Cluegent: ten-post search opportunity batch

Research and publication date: September 14, 2026.

## What the evidence does and does not show

Public search results and competitor pages show which topics competitors target. They do **not** reveal which keywords actually produce their traffic, conversions, or revenue. No Search Console, Ahrefs, or Semrush traffic dataset was available for this analysis. Search volume, keyword difficulty, ranking position, and expected visits are therefore **not measured**, and no traffic increase is guaranteed.

Priorities below are editorial judgments based on commercial relevance, intent specificity, and fit with Cluegent. They are not volume estimates. Competitor articles are used as evidence of topic coverage, not as independent proof of competitor product quality. Product claims in the published comparisons use official product pages and documentation, with a visible publisher disclosure.

## Content and technical implementation

- Ten original articles live in `scripts/seo-growth-posts.mjs`, imported by the existing static-site generator.
- New posts appear first in the blog index. Each has topic-specific metadata, canonical URL, visible date, Article schema, matching FAQ content/schema, contextual internal links, and an entry in the XML sitemap with a truthful modification date.
- Ten existing related guides link back to the new articles. New posts cross-link where useful; the older general question banks retain their separate intent.
- Every post uses the approved product hero above the article. The article has the page's single H1.
- No paid backlinks, fabricated testing, keyword stuffing, hidden text, or automatic indexing claims.
- Comparison pages disclose that Cluegent is the publisher. Product prices are not frozen into comparisons; users are directed to current terms.
- Existing download/free-trial analytics hooks can support conversion analysis, but collection and conversion attribution have not been verified in the analytics account.

## Indexing handoff

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and select the verified `cluegent.com` property.
2. Submit or confirm [the sitemap](https://www.cluegent.com/sitemap.xml) in Sitemaps.
3. Inspect each new article URL and request indexing where available. Sitemap submission and indexing requests do not guarantee indexing or rankings.
4. Review Page indexing and Google's selected canonical if a URL is excluded. Do not create duplicate pages to work around an exclusion.

## Measurement plan—not a scheduled automation

Record the pre-batch 28-day baseline from Search Console and analytics when account access is available. At roughly two and four weeks after publication, inspect index coverage and page-level impressions. At four and eight weeks, compare page/query clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, and relevant download or trial activity, accounting for other site changes and seasonal effects.

- Impressions with low CTR: inspect the actual query intent and competing snippets before editing title/description.
- Relevant impressions but weak positions: improve the specific missing explanation or evidence; avoid arbitrary word-count expansion.
- Multiple Cluegent URLs competing for the same query: review intent and canonicalization before merging or redirecting anything.
- Organic clicks without product engagement: inspect landing-page relevance and CTA clarity; first verify analytics tracking.
- No indexing: check response status, robots, canonical, sitemap inclusion, internal discovery, and content quality before adding more articles.

Confirmed competitor traffic winners require an authorized analytics/export dataset. This batch is a measured content experiment, not a forecast of guaranteed traffic.
