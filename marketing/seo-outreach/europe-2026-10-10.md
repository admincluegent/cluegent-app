# Europe interview preparation campaign — 10 October 2026

## Audience and limits

25 pages: one navigational preparation guide and 24 original worked exercises. English-speaking applicants in the UK, Ireland, Germany, Netherlands, Sweden and Denmark. The UK is geographically European; this campaign is not limited to EU member states. English-language pages do not claim native-language coverage or create local business locations.

No traffic, first-page ranking, hiring or indexing guarantee. Keyword selection is based on qualitative search intent and product fit, not verified search volume. No paid tools, link purchases, automated community posting or fabricated testimonials. Existing Parakeet CTR and US recruiter experiments remain untouched.

## Implementation and release scope

- Descriptive titles/meta descriptions, one H1, self-canonical, stable published/modified dates, visible FAQ answers with matching schema, source links and optional Cluegent CTA.
- Existing homepage-style blog header retained and excluded from the article snippet through the established `data-nosnippet` wrapper.
- Blog navigation highlights Europe; all new pages appear in the blog index. Hub links every exercise; each exercise links back and to another guide.
- Sitemap includes all 25 canonical URLs, with actual publication date. No fake `en-EU` hreflang or IP redirects: this is one English collection, not translated equivalents.
- Generate with `node scripts/generate-seo-site.mjs --europe`; validate with `node scripts/check-europe-seo.mjs --check-protected`.
- Scoped production release changes only 27 files: 25 new pages, blog index and sitemap. Existing production manifest files are retained and verified.
- IndexNow batch includes the 25 pages and updated blog index. A successful receipt is not an indexing confirmation. Google discovers pages through the submitted sitemap; do not use the Google Indexing API for ordinary blog articles.

## Measurement and next markets

No Europe Search Console baseline has been claimed. After release, filter Web performance by each target country and the new page set. Save clicks, impressions, CTR and position for an initial 28-day period; compare a following equal period and inspect device/query mix. This is our review schedule, not Google's requirement or proof of causation. Check signups/downloads separately: an impression is not a click and a click is not a customer.

Use page-by-query data to decide which articles need better snippets or deeper examples. Review important indexing exclusions before writing another bulk batch. Do not treat a sitemap receipt or IndexNow HTTP 200 as proof that all pages are indexed.

Recommended next English-language tests: Canada, Australia, then Singapore. This is an editorial/product-fit recommendation, not a measured traffic ranking. Within Europe, test France and Spain only with native-language research and editorial review; avoid mass translating the whole library without checking intent. Prioritise countries that show actual qualified clicks and signups in our data over assumptions about competitor visits.
