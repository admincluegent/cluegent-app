## Implementation

Twenty original guides each include five interview questions with explained answers, a worked scenario, a practice exercise, two specific FAQs and an official reference. Body length is at least 595 words. Guides connect to established parent pages and complementary new guides, then offer a preparation path to Cluegent. All examples are original; arithmetic and case assumptions are explicit. The approved blog header remains in place.

Each page has a unique title, description, one H1, self-canonical, Article/FAQ structured data, publication date and sitemap entry. FAQ markup does not promise a Google rich result. New parent backlinks are included in the scoped deployment. Deployment retains other production files and hosting configuration.
