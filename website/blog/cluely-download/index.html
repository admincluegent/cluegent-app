Cluely Download: Mac and Windows Requirements and Setup

Use the official Cluely download page, confirm system requirements, understand which plan contains the feature you need, and test everything in a private meeting.

Reviewed by Cluegent Editorial Team · Updated September 25, 2026

Where to download Cluely

Use Cluely's official download page for the macOS or Windows installer. Avoid third-party mirrors, modified installers, and shared accounts. Check the domain and confirm the installer matches your operating system and processor.

Current system requirements

Cluely's official page currently lists macOS 10.15 or later with Apple Silicon or Intel support, and Windows 11 on a 64-bit processor. It also lists 500 MB of free disk space and recommends 8 GB of RAM. Recheck before downloading because requirements change.

Desktop versus mobile

Cluely also publishes a mobile app, but desktop and mobile features and billing may differ. Decide whether you need desktop meeting capture, mobile notes, real-time answers, or cross-device access, then confirm that one subscription covers the surfaces you expect.

Permissions to review

A meeting assistant may request microphone, screen recording, accessibility, notification, or file permissions depending on platform and features. Grant only what the workflow requires. Review privacy terms and avoid uploading confidential employer or personal documents unnecessarily.

Test before upgrading

Use a private Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams call. Check microphone capture, transcription, answer delay, note quality, CPU and battery use, keyboard shortcuts, and reconnect behavior. If screen-share behavior matters, test full-screen, window, tab, and recording separately.

Understand the plan boundary

The official pricing page currently separates free Starter, regular Pro, and Pro plus Undetectability. Installing the desktop app does not mean every feature is free. Check the current tier comparison and cancellation terms before payment.

Use interview AI responsibly

Use any assistant for preparation or only in live sessions where outside help, transcription, screenshots, and recording are permitted. Test the complete setup in a private mock call, protect personal data, and answer from your real experience. No product can guarantee invisibility, an offer, or compliance with every employer's rules.
