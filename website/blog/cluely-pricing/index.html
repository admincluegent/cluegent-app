Cluely Pricing: What Each Plan Includes in 2026

Cluely separates general AI and meeting access from its screen-share invisibility feature. Compare the exact tier you need and verify the live checkout before subscribing.

Reviewed by Cluegent Editorial Team · Updated September 25, 2026

Cluely pricing at a glance

At the time of this review, Cluely's official desktop pricing page lists Starter at free, Pro at $19.99 per month, and Pro plus Undetectability at $149.99 per month. Prices, annual offers, taxes, regional availability, and features can change; verify the official page and checkout.

What the free Starter plan includes

The official comparison currently lists limited AI responses and meeting notes, custom instructions, file uploads with limits, and access to past meetings. Use it to test transcription, answer style, setup, and whether the product fits your normal meetings before upgrading.

What Pro adds

The current Pro tier lists unlimited AI responses, unlimited meeting notetaking, access to current AI models, more customization, and priority support. Check fair-use terms and any platform-specific limits rather than assuming unlimited means every workload is unmetered.

The Undetectability tier

Cluely currently places screen-share invisibility in a separate Pro plus Undetectability tier. If that feature is your purchase reason, test the exact operating system, meeting software, share mode, and recording mode. No comparison can guarantee universal behavior across updates.

Mobile pricing is separate

Cluely's App Store listing may show mobile in-app purchases that differ from desktop plans. Confirm whether a purchase covers desktop, mobile, or both, and compare billing periods before subscribing through an app store or the website.

How to decide if Cluely is worth it

List the features you will use each week, test the free tier, and compare the required paid tier with alternatives on total monthly cost, response quality, privacy, support, and cancellation. Do not pay for the highest tier based only on a label.

Use interview AI responsibly

Use any assistant for preparation or only in live sessions where outside help, transcription, screenshots, and recording are permitted. Test the complete setup in a private mock call, protect personal data, and answer from your real experience. No product can guarantee invisibility, an offer, or compliance with every employer's rules.
