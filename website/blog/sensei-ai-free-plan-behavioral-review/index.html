Start with the official session allowance

Sensei AI's official pricing section currently lists a free plan with 15-minute copilot sessions and other preparation features. That duration does not establish how often you can repeat a free session or whether every account has identical access. Check the current account offer for those details. Avoid comparing a time-limited free session with a paid plan as though both provide the same total capacity.

Choose a real behavioral story

Write a story about correcting a release problem, including what you personally did, who else helped and what changed afterward. Remove confidential names. Keep uncertain measurements labeled as estimates. A behavioral assistant should help organize this material, not create a heroic story with invented revenue, headcount or ownership. Your reference notes provide the ground truth against which an attractive answer can be checked.

Ask for STAR without losing meaning

Request a concise situation, task, action and result answer, then ask which fact supports the result. The response should connect your action to an outcome without implying that correlation proves sole responsibility. Check that the task differs from the action and that the situation is not longer than the explanation. This is an original practice rubric, not a hands-on performance score assigned to Sensei AI.

Challenge the answer

Follow up with what would you do differently and what did your teammate contribute. Look for accountability and fair attribution. Then ask for a shorter answer without removing the limitation you provided. A useful assistant preserves truth while reducing length. If the short version upgrades your role or invents a number, note that error and revise your prompt rather than memorizing the more impressive wording.

Interpret Sensei AI Reddit searches carefully

Check that a post refers to the interview copilot at the official domain, since Sensei is used by unrelated products. Record the plan, session date and actual example. A testimonial about getting an offer is not evidence that software caused the outcome. Treat missing setup details as uncertainty. Seek concrete examples you can reproduce instead of a universal claim that the tool works for every profession.

Compare the same story in Cluegent

Use the same sanitized notes, answer length and follow-up questions in each assistant. Record factual corrections, useful structure changes and whether the final answer sounds like you. Confirm current access conditions rather than assuming the two free offers match. Evaluate the process separately from any generated resume or other bundled feature; your immediate goal is a truthful explanation you can deliver without reading.

Decide whether longer access is useful

After the short evaluation, ask whether the limitation is time, answer quality or your own unclear story. Paying for more time will not fix invented source facts or a missing outcome. If the exercise is useful, examine the current paid-plan commitment and cancellation conditions before buying. Keep practicing the follow-ups independently so you can respond honestly when an interviewer challenges the prepared structure.
