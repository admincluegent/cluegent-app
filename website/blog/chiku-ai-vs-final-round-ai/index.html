Chiku AI vs Final Round AI: Focused Assistant or Career Suite?

Chiku AI centers real-time interview assistance with usage-based plans, while Final Round AI combines preparation tools with a paid live Interview Copilot. Compare the parts you will genuinely use.

Reviewed by Cluegent Editorial Team · Updated September 25, 2026

Quick comparison

Chiku AI's public site emphasizes live answers, screen context, many languages, coding help, and time-based plans. Final Round AI presents a broader preparation and career workflow, with its live Interview Copilot included in paid access. Current plan details can change, so verify both official pages.

Preparation before the call

Final Round AI markets preparation features alongside its live copilot. Chiku's public product focuses more heavily on the in-call assistant and resume or job context. Compare mock depth, feedback, resume tailoring, question generation, and whether your existing preparation process already covers those needs.

During the interview

Run the same mock call with behavioral, technical, and follow-up questions. Measure transcription accuracy, response delay, relevance to your resume, answer length, and how easily you can continue speaking after an interruption. Multi-language claims should be tested in the language and accent you will actually use.

Pricing model

Chiku's terms publish a limited free tier and time-based paid allowances. Final Round AI states that its free plan can be used to explore preparation while live Interview Copilot sessions require paid Pro access. Convert both into cost per realistic interview hour and include unused access, renewal, and cancellation.

Platform checks

Test the specific combination of macOS or Windows, Zoom or Meet or Teams, microphone routing, full-screen or window sharing, and any coding platform. Product pages cannot predict every operating-system permission or meeting-app update.

Which should you choose?

Chiku may fit users who want a focused, time-metered interview assistant and broad language support. Final Round AI may fit users who value an integrated preparation suite around the live copilot. Use a mock interview and a written cost comparison before deciding.

Use interview AI responsibly

Use any assistant for preparation or only in live sessions where outside help, transcription, screenshots, and recording are permitted. Test the complete setup in a private mock call, protect personal data, and answer from your real experience. No product can guarantee invisibility, an offer, or compliance with every employer's rules.
