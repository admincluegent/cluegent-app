Review the current product category

Cluely's current homepage presents a meeting assistant with notes and real-time answers. A historic interview-focused discussion may therefore describe a different emphasis from the product you see today. Start by deciding whether you need meeting recall, actionable notes or interview preparation. This source-checked guide does not equate every meeting feature with interview coaching or assume an older viral demonstration represents today's app.

Separate notes from answer assistance

A meeting summary and an answer suggestion solve different problems. Notes should accurately record decisions and uncertainty after the conversation. Answer help should use relevant context while you work. Evaluate these independently rather than giving a single rating for everything. A polished summary does not prove that live suggestions are accurate, and a good one-off suggestion does not prove that action items are attributed to the correct person.

Use an ambiguous meeting scenario

In a permitted practice conversation, discuss two delivery dates and reject the first one. Assign a research task but leave the owner undecided. Ask for the final decision and the remaining open question. The useful result should not turn an abandoned proposal into a commitment or invent an owner. Keep a written ground truth before starting so you can compare the notes against what was actually agreed.

Add an interview-specific follow-up

Now explain a project and ask for a concise trade-off answer. Change one requirement halfway through and check whether the response updates. This tests a different use case from note capture. If your goal is practice, evaluate whether the suggestion helps you understand your own explanation. Do not infer suitability for every interview from a successful meeting recap; the questions and acceptable assistance policies differ.

Check Cluely free access directly

The official site links to pricing and includes a free-access FAQ, but this guide does not infer unlimited use from the download button. Check the current account restrictions for answer assistance, notes and any platform-specific features. Record whether your exercise is possible without payment. A free download, a limited plan and a recurring allowance are three different things; distinguish them before comparing with another tool.

What to look for in Cluely Reddit reviews

Look for whether the discussion concerns current meeting features or earlier interview positioning. Separate complaints about notes, compatibility and billing. A thread about one app platform does not establish performance on another. Treat concealment claims as vendor or user claims, not permission to break interview rules. Check account settings and consent requirements rather than making a purchasing decision from a provocative headline.

Compare with Cluegent by your actual task

If you need interview preparation, compare the project explanation exercise in both tools. If you need meeting notes, investigate that workflow separately and do not assume every alternative has identical coverage. Write a simple decision statement: the tool is suitable for this task because of these observed results, with these limits. Keep official pricing links and your test notes so that you can revisit the decision when the products change.
