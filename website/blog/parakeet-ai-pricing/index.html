Calculate nominal capacity before comparing value

At the listed rate, 3 credits correspond to six 30-minute blocks, or 180 minutes. That is nominal capacity of 3 hours for the $59 pack if all credits go to calls. It is not a guarantee about rounding, interruptions or effective usable minutes. Ask support about those rules before treating this arithmetic as your final cost.

A ten-minute evaluation plan

  1. Before the session: prepare a sanitized project summary and a small original code example. Configure audio permissions.
  2. Minutes 0–2: verify the transcript using a sentence with a technical term and a correction.
  3. Minutes 2–5: ask for a short project explanation. Check for invented experience.
  4. Minutes 5–8: change a constraint and ask for a revised answer.
  5. Minutes 8–10: examine your remaining allowance and decide what is still untested.

This is a suggested time box, not a test result or a promise about charging behavior. If the meter runs during setup, adapt the plan. Choose one essential uncertainty rather than trying every feature in a short session.
