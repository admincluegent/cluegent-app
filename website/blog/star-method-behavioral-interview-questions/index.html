How to Answer Behavioral Interview Questions With STAR

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, Result. It keeps behavioral answers specific without turning them into long speeches.

Situation

Give enough context for the interviewer to understand the stakes, but keep it short.

Task and action

Explain what you owned and what you actually did. Avoid making the whole answer about the team if the question asks about you.

Result

End with measurable impact, learning, or what changed because of your action.
