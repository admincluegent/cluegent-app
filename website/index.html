Frequently asked questions

What is Cluegent?

Cluegent is a desktop AI meeting assistant that listens when you ask it to, keeps rolling context, and gives real-time answers through a private overlay.

Does Cluegent join my meeting?

No. Cluegent runs as a desktop app. It does not appear as a meeting participant or install a browser extension into the call.

Which platforms does it work with?

It is designed for desktop calls and screen workflows, including Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack Huddles, browser tabs, coding editors, and documents.

Is my data safe?

Cluegent is built on privacy. Recent meeting history and saved local context stay on your machine, while active transcription, prompt, and screenshot requests are processed only when you use those features.

Can I talk to customer support?

Yes. Our support team is available to help you get the most out of your AI meeting assistant. You can reach us at admincluegent@gmail.com.
