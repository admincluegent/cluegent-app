Google Meet setup checklist

Launch Cluegent before opening the Meet call, add your resume context, and test in a private practice meeting. Keep the overlay away from Meet captions, the participant rail, and bottom controls. If you expect to present, test whether you will share a tab, a window, or the entire screen because those are different capture paths.

Voice, type, and screenshot workflows

Listening mode is useful for permitted spoken questions. Typed prompts are faster when you need a rewrite, example, or shorter answer. Screenshot mode is the better choice for visible code, system-design diagrams, stack traces, or questions shared in the Meet chat.

Google Meet troubleshooting

If answers lack context, verify the selected microphone and system permissions, then restart listening. If screen content is incomplete, resize the source window so the full question is visible before taking a screenshot. Customize listening responses to be concise and easy to speak.

Presenting safely and responsibly

Cluegent does not join Meet as a participant. Its private overlay is designed around standard screen-sharing behavior, not every monitoring method. Use it only when AI assistance, transcription, and screenshots are permitted by the interviewer, organization, platform, and applicable law.

Customize Cluegent for your context

Resume context

Listening responses

Screen and type responses

Frequently asked questions

What is Cluegent?

Cluegent is a customizable Windows and macOS desktop AI assistant for live interviews, meetings, coding screens, and screen workflows. It listens when you start it, keeps rolling context, and shows private responses on your desktop.

Does Cluegent join my meeting?

No. Cluegent runs as a desktop app and does not join as a meeting participant or install a browser extension into the call.

Which platforms does Cluegent work with?

Cluegent is designed for desktop workflows across Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack Huddles, coding editors, browsers, and document windows.

Can I customize how Cluegent answers?

Yes. In Settings, the Customize section lets you add resume context, tune listening responses, screen responses, and typed prompt responses, and manage quick action buttons for common prompts.

Is the private overlay a guarantee against every detection method?

No. Cluegent is designed to stay out of standard screen capture behavior, but users are responsible for following laws, platform rules, workplace policies, school policies, and interview rules.
