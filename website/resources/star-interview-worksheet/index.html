Resource FAQ

How to use this template

What does STAR stand for in interviews?

STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, and Result. It helps you explain the context, your responsibility, what you personally did, and what happened afterward.

How long should a STAR answer be?

Most STAR answers work well in 60 to 120 seconds. Keep the situation brief and spend most of the time on your actions, decisions, and result.

What if my result has no metric?

Use truthful observable evidence such as a decision made, risk prevented, process improved, customer response, lesson learned, or a change adopted by the team. Do not invent a number.
