Resource FAQ

How to use this template

How should I score a mock interview?

Use the same criteria after every practice session, score specific observable behavior, and add one example explaining each low or high rating.

What is a good mock interview score?

The trend matters more than one total. A balanced score with clear evidence and honest follow-up handling is more useful than confident delivery with weak examples.

Can an interviewer use this scorecard?

Yes. A peer, mentor, career coach, or study partner can use it during a mock interview and print the completed feedback afterward.
