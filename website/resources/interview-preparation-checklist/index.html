Resource FAQ

How to use this template

When should I start preparing for an interview?

Begin as soon as the interview is scheduled. Use the first session for company and role research, then spend the remaining time practising evidence and realistic follow-up questions.

What should I prepare the day before an interview?

Confirm the time, link or location, interviewer names, documents, device setup, clothing, travel plan, and two or three concise questions to ask at the end.

Can I print this interview checklist?

Yes. Use the print button to print the checklist or save it as a PDF from your browser's print dialog.
