## Summary of Cluegent Modifications

Cluegent includes modifications and additions for:

- Cluegent product branding and desktop UI changes.
- Firebase authentication, backend Functions, Firestore entitlement state, and usage tracking.
- Server-managed speech-to-text and LLM integrations.
- Screenshot analysis and AI response behavior customization.
- Razorpay subscription billing flows and plan enforcement.
- Website, privacy policy, terms, refund/cancellation policy, and acceptable-use policy.
- Local meeting history and local app customization storage.
- Production-readiness updates for plans, consent, account deletion, and support links.
